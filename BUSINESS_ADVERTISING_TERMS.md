# Business and Advertising Terms — Pre-publication Draft

Last updated: 2026-09-02. No commercial rate, country/category activation, settlement provider or legal approval is created by this draft.

Partner participation follows application, evidence-based verification, contract, technical validation, approval and activation. Submission alone never activates a partner. Each versioned agreement defines its commercial model, qualifying event, rate/value, currency, attribution window, effective period, refunds, settlement, geography and limits; historical rules are not rewritten.

Organic ranking follows user intent, hard constraints and verified relevance, price, quality, availability, delivery, distance/time and reliability. Commission cannot secretly improve organic position, and a useful non-partner result cannot be hidden merely because it pays no commission. Sponsored placement is separate, conspicuously disclosed, and cannot masquerade as the organic best result.

Partners must authenticate feeds/webhooks, provide accurate source timestamps, protect credentials, avoid replay/fraud/self-referral and preserve evidence for refunds, disputes and reconciliation. Stale or partner-provided data is not represented as verified live. Suspension stops new monetizable handoffs while required audit, refund and settlement history remains.

UniversalAssistant is not a bank, payment processor, accounting suite, KYC provider, seller, or merchant of record. Production terms require owner/legal approval for jurisdiction, regulated category, privacy, taxes, invoicing, sponsored disclosure and settlement.
