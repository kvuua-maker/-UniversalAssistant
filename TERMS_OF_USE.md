# UniversalAssistant Terms of Use — Pre-publication Draft

Last updated: 2026-09-02. Owner/legal approval and publication are required before release.

UniversalAssistant assists with discovery, comparison, local Apple actions and handoff to external providers. Live facts such as price, availability, weather, routes and booking state come only from identified providers and may be partial, stale, unavailable or change after display. Users must review consequential actions before completing them.

At launch, UniversalAssistant is discovery/referral/attribution infrastructure, not the seller or merchant of record for external goods or services. The transaction is between the user and the identified seller/service provider. UniversalAssistant does not take purchase funds, control inventory/fulfillment, or guarantee a provider's service.

Users must use the service lawfully and may not abuse providers, fabricate conversions, bypass access controls, replay webhooks, interfere with availability, or use emergency features as a substitute for official emergency services. Third-party services remain subject to their own terms and availability.

Subscriptions, if activated, use the disclosed StoreKit product and entitlement behavior. Sponsored results must be clearly labeled and separate from organic ranking. Commission does not secretly improve organic ranking.
