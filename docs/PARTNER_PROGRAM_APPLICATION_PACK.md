# UniversalAssistant Partner Program Application Pack

Status: locally prepared; publication and applications require owner-controlled legal/business identity, public URLs and provider accounts.

## Product description

UniversalAssistant is an iPhone action/discovery assistant that returns provider-neutral, verified results and hands users to exact authorized provider destinations. It is not merchant of record for external goods or services. Organic ranking is independent of commission.

## Integration summary

- Normalized offer/service models preserve provider, source, freshness, exact destination and attribution evidence.
- Affiliate parameters are allowlisted per provider; generic results never invent attribution.
- Conversion and exact-Decimal commission ledgers are idempotent, auditable and refund-adjusted.
- Signed webhooks, replay protection, tenant isolation and server-side secret boundaries are implemented.

## Required owner-controlled application material

Before any submission, the owner must provide or approve the legal business identity and contact, public product landing URL, published Privacy Policy and Terms URLs, support URL/email, truthful screenshots/demo, markets/categories, and each program-specific commercial representation. No placeholder is submitted as fact.

## Application evidence

For every network/merchant retain application reference, effective program terms/version, approved tracking mechanisms, status and subsequent test/production conversion evidence in the typed program registry. `EARNING_VERIFIED` requires conversion-attribution evidence; a click is insufficient.
