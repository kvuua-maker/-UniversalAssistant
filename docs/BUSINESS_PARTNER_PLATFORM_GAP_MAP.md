# Business Partner Platform Gap Map

Reuse without reopening: `Money`/Decimal, normalized offers and product identity, provider capability/authentication boundaries, affiliate purchase attribution state, organic ranking independent of commission, bounded provider fan-out, APNs/job durability, account policy, sync reconciliation, export/deletion and privacy manifest.

Future M9 blocks, after the active coordination-addendum sequence:

1. Partner domain, onboarding state machine, verification and audited transitions.
2. Versioned commercial agreements and configuration-driven monetization models.
3. Attribution/conversion lifecycle plus append-only Decimal commission ledger and refund adjustments.
4. Signed idempotent webhook ingestion, replay defense and secret rotation boundary.
5. Reconciliation and settlement-state core without payment processing.
6. Tenant authorization/isolation for partner dashboard and internal admin.
7. Sponsored/organic separation and disclosure enforcement.
8. Feed freshness, health, bounded query/failure isolation and offboarding.
9. M9 acceptance cases A–P.

Owner-controlled production policy (categories, countries, commercial values, legal templates, settlement/tax providers, sponsored policy and Business Pro pricing) remains an explicit external decision and is not invented.
