BUSINESS PARTNER PLATFORM — FINAL CANONICAL ADDENDUM
For UniversalAssistant / Master Spec v13.2

STATUS: This addendum supplements Master Spec v13.2 and every accepted addendum. It does not reopen M0–M8 or authorize duplicate provider, commerce, capability, affiliate, attribution, security, authentication or analytics components.

1. BUSINESS GOAL: support voluntary direct-business onboarding through application, verification, agreement, integration, technical validation, approval, activation, discovery, attribution, conversion, refund/reconciliation and settlement, from small local businesses to international providers.
2. LEGAL/COMMERCIAL POSITION: launch as discovery/comparison/referral/attribution infrastructure, not seller or merchant of record. User and partner retain the transaction relationship. No custody of purchase funds, fulfillment, inventory, delivery, resale or hidden markup without separate owner/legal approval. Eligibility is policy/config driven.
3. PARTNER CATEGORIES: normalized architecture supports lawful commerce, hospitality, travel, transport, events, local/professional/logistics/education services; regulated/high-risk categories require separate gates.
4. ENTRY/MONETIZATION: basic participation may be free. Contract-configured models are percent/fixed confirmed outcomes, qualified leads/services, new-customer bounty, affiliate commission, Business subscription, disclosed sponsored placement or custom contract. Never hard-code commercial values.
5. INTEGRATION LEVELS: L1 verified basic profile; L2 timestamped feed/catalog; L3 affiliate/deep-link attribution; L4 permitted full API/webhook/reconciliation. Partners need not share one level.
6. SELF-SERVICE ONBOARDING: DRAFT, SUBMITTED, VERIFICATION_REQUIRED, UNDER_REVIEW, TECHNICAL_VALIDATION, CONTRACT_REQUIRED, APPROVED, ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, REJECTED, TERMINATED. Submission alone never activates; every transition is audited.
7. VERIFICATION: never claim verified without evidence; country/category/law/model/risk determine requirements and critical changes may trigger re-verification.
8. VERSIONED AGREEMENTS: immutable historical ID/version/effective period/model/rate/currency/attribution/refund/settlement/status; each conversion uses the applicable event-time version.
9. ORGANIC INTEGRITY: commission never secretly improves organic rank or hides a better lawful non-partner result. Rank by verified user-outcome criteria.
10. SPONSORED: separate from organic, explicitly disclosed by locale/law, relevance-preserving and without dark patterns.
11. BUSINESS PRO: future optional analytics/automation/team/feed/API/reporting tools; never mandatory for basic organic participation unless owner changes policy; no invented price.
12. ATTRIBUTION: minimize data; use attribution/partner/source/offer/campaign IDs, pseudonymous reference where appropriate, expiry and agreement version; no hidden cross-service tracking.
13. CONVERSION STATES: INITIATED, PENDING, CONFIRMED, REJECTED, CANCELLED, REFUNDED, PARTIALLY_REFUNDED, PAYABLE, PAID, DISPUTED. A click earns nothing unless its contract explicitly says so.
14. COMMISSION LEDGER: exact decimal money, original evidence and append/adjustment audit history; never binary floating point or erased financial truth.
15. REFUNDS/DISPUTES: full/partial refund, cancellation, no-show, applicable chargeback, invalid/duplicate/fraud outcomes preserve evidence, actor, reason, time, status, resolution and adjustments.
16. WEBHOOK SECURITY: authenticated signatures, replay defense, idempotent event IDs, duplicate/schema checks, audit logs and server-side rotatable secrets only.
17. RECONCILIATION: compare internal attribution/conversion with partner/network reports and expose missing, duplicate, amount/currency/status/refund mismatches without silent rewrite.
18. SETTLEMENT: do not build a bank/payment processor. PAYABLE is not PAID before confirmation. Invoice, settlement and tax metadata are jurisdiction/config/provider scoped; no merchant/tax-agent claim without owner/legal decision.
19. MULTI-CURRENCY: preserve original and commission currencies plus historical FX source/rate/time; never recalculate history using current FX.
20. PARTNER DASHBOARD: strict tenant scope; truthful observed/attributed/confirmed/estimated/unavailable profile, feed, health, traffic, conversion, commission, refund, settlement and error metrics.
21. INTERNAL ADMIN: authorized/audited application, verification, moderation, agreement, integration, conversion, dispute, reconciliation, settlement, fraud and sponsored controls; high-risk actions require stronger access/audit.
22. TENANT ISOLATION: backend/data authorization prevents cross-partner credential, analytics, conversion, contract and finance access; UI-only isolation is insufficient.
23. DATA FRESHNESS: VERIFIED_LIVE, PARTNER_PROVIDED_RECENT, STALE, UNKNOWN with source time; stale/manual data never appears live.
24. SMALL/LARGE BUSINESS: verified basic profiles work without fake live claims; full developer platform capabilities are added only when justified.
25. FRAUD/ABUSE: defend against fabricated/duplicate/self-referral/replay/bot/refund/lead/commission/data manipulation with auditable decisions.
26. CONTENT/COMPLAINTS: validate/moderate content; complaints are review signals, not automatic guilt.
27. HEALTH/FAILURE: ACTIVE_HEALTHY, DEGRADED, STALE_DATA, TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE, SUSPENDED; bounded timeout/concurrency/rate/retry/backoff/circuit-breaking and isolated truthful alternatives.
28. OFFBOARDING: suspension/termination stops new monetizable handoffs, revokes credentials as appropriate and preserves required audit/accounting plus pending refund/settlement processing.
29. PERFORMANCE/SCALE: B2B never slows deterministic fast path; indexed/paginated/bounded queries, cache and streaming/batch replace all-partner RAM loading or app-binary hardcoding.
30. NORMALIZED ADAPTERS: direct partners and networks map into common Partner/Offer/Service/results while preserving source evidence and network-specific attribution.
31. USER TRANSPARENCY: show actual seller/provider and required affiliate/commission disclosure; sponsored disclosure is mandatory; monetization never changes facts.
32. OWNER CONTROL: owner decides categories, models, templates, approvals, sponsored policy, Business Pro, countries, settlement and compliance versions. Codex does not invent them.
33. NO OVERENGINEERING: no proprietary bank, processor, accounting suite, CRM, ad exchange or KYC provider; reuse boundaries and history.
34. ACCEPTANCE: test activation gates, tenant isolation, ranking independence, sponsored separation, webhook idempotency, refund adjustments, historical agreements, freshness, failure isolation, reconciliation, suspension, non-partner inclusion, exact money, secret absence, dashboard isolation and contract-configurable models.
35. IMPLEMENTATION ORDER: persist/reference/hash, gap-analyze and add only missing future B2B blocks; continue the current first unfinished block instead of prematurely building all B2B.
36. FINAL CONTRACT: monetize via verified outcomes, future optional Business Pro, disclosed sponsorship and consumer subscription; organic usefulness and trust outrank commission; launch remains discovery/referral infrastructure.
37. CONTINUATION: for every block inspect, implement, build, test, fix, verify, update, commit, push and continue; record true owner-only blockers and pursue independent work; owner-facing communication is Ukrainian.
