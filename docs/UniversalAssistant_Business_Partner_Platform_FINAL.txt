BUSINESS PARTNER PLATFORM — FINAL CANONICAL ADDENDUM For
UniversalAssistant / Master Spec v13.2

STATUS This addendum supplements, and does not replace, Master Spec
v13.2, Marketplace Commerce & Affiliate Hub, Deterministic Multi-Step
Coordination + Bounded AI Fallback, and all accepted addenda. Do not
restart the project, reopen completed M0–M7 or completed M8 blocks, or
duplicate existing
provider/commerce/capability/affiliate/attribution/security/authentication/analytics
components. Inspect actual code/state first, reuse what exists,
implement only gaps, schedule B2B work in the correct future milestone,
and continue the current roadmap.

1.  BUSINESS GOAL Support direct businesses that voluntarily apply to
    appear in UniversalAssistant, in addition to affiliate networks and
    existing providers. Lifecycle: application → verification →
    commercial agreement → integration → technical validation → approval
    → activation → discovery → verified attribution → conversion →
    commission → refund/cancellation → reconciliation → settlement.
    Scale from a local small business to an international provider.

2.  LEGAL/COMMERCIAL POSITION Initial model: UniversalAssistant is
    discovery/comparison/referral/attribution infrastructure, not seller
    or merchant of record for physical goods/external services. USER ↔
    PARTNER remains the primary transaction relationship. Do not take
    user purchase funds, fulfillment, inventory, delivery, resale or
    hidden markup without a separate owner/legal decision.
    Country/category legal eligibility is policy/config driven and
    requires appropriate approval before production activation.

3.  PARTNER CATEGORIES Architecture must support marketplaces, stores,
    brands, restaurants, food delivery, grocery, hotels, travel,
    airlines, rail/bus/tickets, taxi/rides, car rental, events, salons,
    repair/automotive, local professionals, logistics,
    education/services and other lawful categories. Regulated/high-risk
    categories require separate compliance gates.

4.  ENTRY AND MONETIZATION Basic participation may be free of mandatory
    monthly fee; primary launch monetization is verified-result based.
    Do not hardcode prices. Supported contract-driven models:
    PERCENT_OF_CONFIRMED_SALE FIXED_PER_CONFIRMED_ORDER
    FIXED_PER_CONFIRMED_BOOKING FIXED_PER_CONFIRMED_GUEST
    FIXED_PER_QUALIFIED_LEAD FIXED_PER_COMPLETED_SERVICE
    NEW_CUSTOMER_BOUNTY AFFILIATE_NETWORK_COMMISSION
    BUSINESS_SUBSCRIPTION SPONSORED_PLACEMENT CUSTOM_CONTRACT Agreement
    defines rate/value, currency, qualifying event, attribution window,
    dates, refunds, settlement, geography, scope and caps/floors.

5.  INTEGRATION LEVELS L1 BASIC PROFILE: verified identity, category,
    location, hours, contacts, service area, menu/catalog/services,
    provided prices, links/media and freshness. L2 FEED/CATALOG:
    structured products/services, price, availability, variants,
    locations, delivery/service data and timestamps. L3 AFFILIATE/DEEP
    LINK: tracking ID, campaign/sub-ID, exact handoff/deep link,
    attribution and conversion reporting. L4 FULL API: permitted
    search/catalog/price/availability/order-or-booking
    request/state/webhooks/attribution/conversion/reconciliation. Do not
    require the same level from every partner.

6.  SELF-SERVICE ONBOARDING Application → business/legal identity →
    contacts/registration → settlement info when required →
    category/geography → integration method → commercial model/agreement
    → technical test → review → approval → active. A submitted form
    alone never activates a business. States: DRAFT, SUBMITTED,
    VERIFICATION_REQUIRED, UNDER_REVIEW, TECHNICAL_VALIDATION,
    CONTRACT_REQUIRED, APPROVED, ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, REJECTED,
    TERMINATED. Audit every transition.

7.  VERIFICATION Never label a business verified without evidence.
    Verification requirements depend on country, category, law,
    transaction model and risk. Critical verified/legal-field changes
    may require re-verification.

8.  VERSIONED COMMERCIAL AGREEMENTS Keep agreement ID/version/effective
    period/model/rate/currency/attribution/refund/settlement/status.
    Never retroactively rewrite historical commission rules. Each
    conversion uses the contract version applicable to its qualifying
    event.

9.  ORGANIC RANKING INTEGRITY HARD RULE: partner commission must not
    secretly improve organic ranking. Organic ranking follows user
    intent/constraints and verified relevance, match, final price,
    quality, availability, delivery, distance, time, legitimate
    reliability/rating and task-specific criteria. A higher-paying
    partner cannot displace a better organic result merely because it
    pays more. Do not hide a useful lawful non-partner result merely
    because it generates no commission.

10. SPONSORED RESULTS Sponsored placement is separate from organic
    ranking, explicitly disclosed as sponsored/advertising according to
    locale/legal rules, and never masquerades as the organic best
    result. Campaign data may include budget, targeting, dates, billing
    and reporting. No dark patterns.

11. BUSINESS PRO Architecturally allow future paid business tools such
    as advanced analytics, automation, teams, advanced
    feeds/API/reporting/insights. Do not make Business Pro mandatory for
    basic organic participation unless owner changes policy. Do not
    invent pricing.

12. ATTRIBUTION Where lawful/needed, monetizable handoff has
    attribution_id, partner_id, source/provider, pseudonymous reference
    when appropriate, offer/service reference, campaign/sub-ID if
    applicable, creation/expiry and agreement version. Minimize personal
    data; no hidden cross-service tracking.

13. CONVERSION LIFECYCLE INITIATED, PENDING, CONFIRMED, REJECTED,
    CANCELLED, REFUNDED, PARTIALLY_REFUNDED, PAYABLE, PAID, DISPUTED. A
    click is not earned commission unless the contract explicitly pays
    for that event.

14. COMMISSION LEDGER Store conversion/partner/agreement/external
    transaction IDs, legitimate gross amount, currencies, commission
    basis/rate/value/amount, state, timestamps, refund adjustment and
    source evidence. Use exact monetary representation, not binary
    floating point. Financial history is auditable/append-or-adjustment
    based; never erase prior financial truth.

15. REFUNDS AND DISPUTES Handle full/partial refund, cancellation,
    no-show, applicable chargeback, rejected lead,
    duplicate/invalid/fraudulent conversion. Disputes preserve reason,
    evidence, timestamps, status, resolution, adjustments and actor.
    Never delete history to “fix” a dispute.

16. WEBHOOK SECURITY Direct integrations use authenticated/signed
    callbacks, replay protection where appropriate, idempotency, event
    IDs, duplicate detection, schema validation and audit logs. Secrets
    stay server-side secure storage/key management; never client, source
    control or user-visible logs. Support credential
    rotation/revocation.

17. RECONCILIATION Compare internal attribution/conversion ledger with
    partner/network settlement reports. Detect missing/duplicate
    conversions, amount/currency/status mismatches and unexpected
    refunds. Never silently rewrite finance data.

18. SETTLEMENT Do not build a bank/payment processor. Direct partners
    owe confirmed payable commissions according to contract; affiliate
    networks use their payout rules. PAYABLE is not PAID until
    settlement confirmation. Support invoice/settlement references and
    tax metadata, but tax/VAT/invoice logic is
    jurisdiction/config/accounting-provider specific. Do not declare
    UniversalAssistant tax agent/deemed supplier/merchant of record
    without owner/legal decision.

19. MULTI-CURRENCY Preserve original transaction currency, commission
    currency and FX source/rate/time when conversion is required. Never
    recalculate historical accounting using today’s FX.

20. PARTNER DASHBOARD Tenant-scoped dashboard may expose only that
    partner’s allowed profile/integration/feed health, legitimate
    impressions, handoffs, leads, bookings, conversions, rates,
    attributed value where permitted, commissions, refunds,
    payable/paid, API errors and freshness.
    Observed/attributed/confirmed/estimated/unavailable metrics must be
    distinguished truthfully.

21. INTERNAL ADMIN Support partner applications, verification,
    approval/rejection/suspension, agreements/versions, commission
    rules, integrations/health, conversions, disputes, refunds,
    reconciliation, settlement, fraud/anomaly signals, sponsored
    campaigns and audit history. High-risk admin actions require
    appropriate access control/audit.

22. TENANT ISOLATION Backend/data-access enforcement must prevent
    Partner A from reading Partner B credentials, analytics,
    conversions, contracts or finance. UI-only isolation is
    insufficient. Add authorization/isolation tests.

23. DATA FRESHNESS Partner price/availability/menu/catalog/hours/service
    data carries source timestamp/freshness. Distinguish VERIFIED_LIVE,
    PARTNER_PROVIDED_RECENT, STALE and UNKNOWN. Never present
    stale/manual data as verified live.

24. SMALL AND LARGE BUSINESS Small businesses without APIs can use
    verified Basic Profile without false live claims. Large partners can
    later use API docs, sandbox, credentials, webhooks, feeds,
    versioning, idempotency, rate limits, test/production credentials
    and migration/deprecation policy. Do not overbuild a developer
    platform before needed.

25. FRAUD/ABUSE Protect against fake conversions, self-referral,
    duplicate attribution, webhook replay, fabricated leads, bots,
    refund/commission manipulation and partner data tampering.
    Money/status-impacting decisions must be auditable and appropriately
    explainable.

26. CONTENT/COMPLAINTS Partner content is validated/moderated; illegal,
    deceptive, malicious or prohibited listings are not accepted. User
    complaints can reference partner/listing/order where available and
    become review signals, not automatic guilt.

27. HEALTH, LIMITS AND FAILURE ISOLATION Partner health states include
    ACTIVE_HEALTHY, DEGRADED, STALE_DATA, TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE,
    SUSPENDED. Use bounded concurrency, timeout, rate-limit handling,
    retry/backoff/circuit breaking where appropriate. One failing
    partner must not block the Hub or fabricate fallback data; return
    available verified alternatives.

28. OFFBOARDING Suspension/termination stops new monetizable handoffs
    but preserves required accounting/audit history and valid pending
    settlement/refund processing. Revoke credentials where appropriate.

29. PERFORMANCE AND SCALE B2B must not slow deterministic fast path. Do
    not load all partners/catalogs into RAM. Use
    indexed/paginated/bounded queries, valid caching, streaming/batch
    for large feeds. Architecture must scale from tens to
    hundreds/thousands of partners without hardcoding all partners in
    the app binary.

30. NORMALIZED ADAPTER ARCHITECTURE Affiliate networks and direct
    partners adapt into a common normalized Partner/Offer/Service/result
    domain where appropriate. Preserve source evidence and
    network-specific attribution semantics without scattering
    provider-specific logic through UI/domain.

31. USER TRANSPARENCY Show the actual seller/service provider where
    relevant. Sponsored disclosure is mandatory. Affiliate/commission
    disclosure is applied where required. Monetization never changes
    factual answers.

32. OWNER CONTROL Owner retains final control over supported categories,
    monetization models, commercial templates, partner approval,
    sponsored policy, Business Pro, market/country activation,
    settlement providers and legal/compliance policy versions. Codex
    must not invent these business decisions.

33. NO OVERENGINEERING Do not build a proprietary bank, payment
    processor, accounting suite, CRM, ad exchange or KYC provider when
    domain boundaries plus external integrations are sufficient. Do not
    reopen completed milestones merely to rename/restructure working
    code.

34. REQUIRED ACCEPTANCE TESTS A. Application cannot become ACTIVE
    without required verification/approval. B. Partner A cannot access
    Partner B. C. Higher commission does not alter organic ranking. D.
    Sponsored result is explicitly separated. E. Duplicate webhook
    creates no duplicate conversion/commission. F. Full/partial refund
    creates auditable adjustment. G. Historical agreement version
    preserves correct commission. H. Stale price/availability is not
    verified live. I. Partner API failure does not break other
    providers. J. Reconciliation detects missing/duplicate/mismatch. K.
    Suspended partner receives no new monetizable handoffs. L. Better
    non-partner result is not hidden due to zero commission. M. Money
    arithmetic has no floating-point accounting drift. N. Secrets do not
    appear in client/logs/repository. O. Partner dashboard is
    tenant-isolated. P. Commercial model changes through config/contract
    without rewriting core logic.

35. IMPLEMENTATION ORDER Immediately persist this addendum under docs/
    as canonical and record its reference/hash in canonical state.
    Perform gap analysis against actual code. Reuse completed
    functionality. Add only missing B2B blocks to the correct future
    roadmap. Then continue the current first unfinished milestone/block;
    do not stop current roadmap to build the entire B2B platform
    prematurely.

36. FINAL BUSINESS CONTRACT UniversalAssistant should monetize through a
    combination of verified transaction/result commissions, future
    Business Pro, clearly disclosed Sponsored placements and existing
    consumer subscription where applicable. Basic partner entry should
    remain low-friction. Organic usefulness and user trust outrank
    commission size. UniversalAssistant remains discovery/referral
    infrastructure at launch rather than merchant of record unless owner
    later changes that decision.

37. CONTINUATION RULE After accepting and persisting this addendum,
    continue autonomous UniversalAssistant implementation from the
    actual first unfinished block. For every block: inspect actual state
    → implement → build → test → fix → verify → update state/docs →
    commit → push → immediately select next unfinished block. Do not
    repeat completed blocks. Do not stop between checkpoints without a
    real owner-only blocker. If one block is owner-only blocked, record
    the exact blocker and continue another independent unblocked block
    when possible. All owner-facing messages must be Ukrainian.
