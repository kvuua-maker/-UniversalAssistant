## 1. PROJECT PURPOSE

UniversalAssistant is a local-first, iPhone-first universal action/discovery assistant. Its intended interaction is user request → truthful result → legitimate action, rather than a general chat transcript, browser clone, provider dashboard, or AI demo. Simple operations are deterministic and do not require AI. Personal iPhone data remains local/system-owned where Apple frameworks are authoritative; external providers are reached through typed bounded contracts with provenance, freshness and truthful failure behavior.

The launch commercial position is discovery/comparison/referral/attribution infrastructure, not merchant of record for physical goods or external services. The user and seller/provider remain the primary transaction parties unless the owner later makes a separate legal/business decision.

Sources: `ARCHITECTURE.md`; `docs/UniversalAssistant_FULL_UNABRIDGED_MASTER_SPEC_v13.2_MAC_GIT_VERIFIED_FINAL.txt`; Business Partner, visual, coordination and unknown-intent canonical addenda.

## 4. CURRENT ARCHITECTURE

- Native Swift/SwiftUI iOS application, minimum iOS 18 according to `ARCHITECTURE.md` and project configuration.
- Entry: `UniversalAssistantApp` → `AssistantHomeView` / `AssistantViewModel`.

### Evidence governance

- `CapabilityEvidenceGovernance`, `IntegrationVerificationPolicy`, `FeasibilityPathRegistry` and requirement inventory prevent unsupported or over-broad capability claims.
- Evidence levels distinguish local behavior from provider/device verification.
